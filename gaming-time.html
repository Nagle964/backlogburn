Gaming time calculator FAQ

How many hours is 10 hours of gaming per week in a year?

Ten hours per week is about 522 hours per year. That is approximately 21.7 complete 24-hour days.

How does the playtime calculator work?

It multiplies your weekly gaming hours by 52.1775 to estimate yearly playtime. Monthly and daily averages, full days and five-year totals are then calculated from that yearly figure.

Can I use this as a game time calculator for any platform?

Yes. The calculation is platform-independent, so your weekly hours can include Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, Nintendo, PC games, mobile games or a mixture of platforms.

How can I estimate my weekly gaming time?

Use a recent normal week if you know it. If your schedule varies, take your gaming hours across several typical weeks and divide by the number of weeks to get a more representative average.
