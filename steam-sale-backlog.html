Discount does not equal value

This calculator does not tell you whether a game is worth buying. It makes the trade-off visible: the cash price falls during a sale, but every game you genuinely plan to finish also adds time to your backlog.

Use it before a sale checkout

If your cart contains several games, use the average normal price, average discount and a realistic average game length. The result shows how many weeks of your normal backlog time the purchase represents.
