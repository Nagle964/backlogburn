About BacklogBurn

BacklogBurn is a small collection of free browser-based tools for putting numbers on gaming habits: backlog size, completion pace, time and value.

The goal is not to turn gaming into homework. The calculators are planning aids for people who are curious about the gap between the games they own, the games they buy and the time they actually have.

How the site is built

The calculators run locally in the browser and do not require an account. Inputs are not sent to a server by the current static version.
