Games per year calculator FAQ

How is games per year calculated?

The calculator converts weekly gaming time into annual hours, multiplies that by the share of time you use to finish games, then divides by your average game length.

Why include a completion-focus percentage?

Not every gaming hour goes toward finishing a backlog. Multiplayer sessions, replays and games you abandon can use time without increasing the number of completed games.

Can I use an average game length instead of exact lengths?

Yes. The tool is designed for planning, so a realistic average across the kinds of games you normally play is enough for an estimate.
