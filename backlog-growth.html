Why the buying rate matters

You can finish games consistently and still never reduce the backlog if new purchases arrive faster than completed or deliberately abandoned games leave it. The simplest useful measure is backlog burn rate: games out minus games in.

Dropping games counts

A backlog should represent games you genuinely still intend to play. Removing a game you have decided is not worth your time is a legitimate way to reduce it rather than a failed completion.
