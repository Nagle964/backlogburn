FAQ

Game backlog calculator questions

How do I calculate how long my gaming backlog will take?

Estimate the remaining hours in your backlog, then divide that by the number of hours per month you actually spend progressing unfinished games. BacklogBurn also subtracts the hours represented by new games you keep adding.

Why can the calculator say my backlog will never clear?

If the estimated playtime of the games you add each month is greater than the backlog hours you clear each month, the pile grows faster than you can finish it. In that case there is no mathematical clear date unless one of those habits changes.

What should I use for average game length?

Use your own library if you know it. Otherwise pick the preset closest to the kinds of games you normally finish. A backlog of short indie games behaves very differently from one dominated by RPGs.

Does this connect to Steam?

Not in the current version. The calculator intentionally works without an account or API connection, so you can use it immediately and without sharing a gaming profile.
