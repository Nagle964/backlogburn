Steam Autumn Sale 2026: Dates and Buying Guide

The sale runs from 1–8 October 2026. Use this guide to check the dates, assess a purchase before or during the sale, and work out how much playtime it adds to your backlog.

The confirmed Steam Autumn Sale 2026 dates

Valve lists the 2026 Steam Autumn Sale for 1 October to 8 October 2026. Steam-wide seasonal sales are open to released games, but publishers choose whether to enter a discount. That means a sale event does not guarantee that one particular game will become cheaper.

Check Valve’s official seasonal sale dates.

Should you buy now or wait?

The following comparison applies before the sale starts on 1 October. During the sale, use the cart calculator and checkout questions below to assess the offers available.

WAIT FOR THE SALE IF…

There is no reason to buy today

  • You will not start the game before 1 October.
  • The current offer is only a small discount.
  • You already have several games you intend to finish first.
  • You are buying because the offer feels urgent, not because you have time to play.
BUY NOW IF…

The current deal already makes sense

  • You plan to start playing before the Autumn Sale.
  • The present price is already acceptable to you.
  • You have checked the refund terms and platform compatibility.
  • The purchase will not simply disappear into your backlog.

A discount is not the same as a good purchase

A game can be 70% off and still be poor value for you if it remains unplayed. Before buying, compare the money saved with the extra hours added to your backlog and the date you realistically expect to begin.

Four questions to ask before checkout

  1. Will I play it before the next major sale? If not, waiting costs you very little.
  2. How many unfinished games come first? A long queue reduces the practical value of buying now.
  3. Is the price affordable without relying on the claimed saving? Spending less than the list price is still spending money.
  4. How many hours am I adding? A six-game cart can represent months of your available gaming time.

What if the game does not go on sale?

No individual discount is guaranteed. If you would happily play the game immediately at its current price, buying now can be reasonable. If you only want it because a sale might make it feel like a bargain, add it to your wishlist and wait. The aim is not always to obtain the lowest possible price; it is to avoid buying games you will not use.

Make a purchase you have time to play

Before the sale, waiting can make sense if you will not start playing immediately. During the sale, judge the actual price against your budget, available gaming time and unfinished games. A discount is useful only if the purchase fits your plans.
